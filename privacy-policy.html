Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

AstroSolve is operated by Loopforge Labs (OPC) Private Limited ("AstroSolve", "we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the AstroSolve mobile app and website, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to both customers and astrologers using AstroSolve.

By creating an account or using AstroSolve you agree to the processing described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

Who can use AstroSolve

AstroSolve is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your mobile number (verified by OTP), name, and optionally your email address and profile photo.
  • Birth details: date, time, and place of birth, gender, marital status, and preferred language, used to prepare horoscopes, kundli charts, and consultations.
  • Consultation content: chat messages you exchange with astrologers and recordings of voice consultations.
  • Reviews and ratings you post about astrologers.
  • Support requests and other messages you send us.
  • For astrologers: professional profile details (display name, bio, skills, languages, rates), identity and qualification documents, sample recordings, and skill-test results submitted for verification.

Information collected automatically

  • Payments and wallet: top-up amounts, wallet balance, consultation charges, and transaction history. Card, UPI, and bank details are entered directly with our payment partner, Razorpay; we do not see or store them.
  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, a device identifier, push-notification token, and login session details.
  • Analytics: information about how you use the app (for example, screens viewed and features used) to help us understand and improve AstroSolve.
  • Call metadata: when a consultation runs over a phone call, the call time, duration, and connection status.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your phone number.
  • To connect you with astrologers and run chat and voice consultations.
  • To prepare horoscopes and kundli charts from your birth details.
  • To manage your wallet, process payments and refunds, and bill consultations.
  • To send you notifications about bookings, consultations, and payments.
  • To verify astrologers before they can take consultations.
  • To keep the platform safe: preventing fraud and abuse, enforcing our Terms of Service, and resolving disputes and complaints.
  • To analyse usage and improve the app.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Messages we send you. We send service messages about your account, OTPs, bookings, consultations, payments, and refunds; these are part of the service. We may also send offers and updates about AstroSolve. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time by contacting us, or turn off AstroSolve notifications in your phone's settings. Opting out of promotions does not stop service messages.

Aggregated data. We may combine and anonymise data (for example, the number of consultations per day or popular features) so that it cannot reasonably identify you, and use it to understand and improve AstroSolve.

Your phone number stays private

Astrologers do not see your phone number. In-app voice consultations run over the internet, and consultations over a regular phone call are connected through a masked AstroSolve number, so neither side sees the other's personal number.

Call recordings and chats

Voice consultations may be recorded, and chat messages are stored. We use them to resolve disputes and complaints, investigate safety or misconduct reports, and check consultation quality. You can play back recordings of your own consultations in the app. Recordings and chats are available to you, the astrologer you consulted, and authorised AstroSolve staff.

Please share only the information needed for your consultation, and do not share passwords, OTPs, bank details, or contact details with astrologers. Astrologers may use what you tell them only to conduct your consultation on AstroSolve, and must not contact you outside the platform. If an astrologer asks for personal or payment details, report it to us.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed to run AstroSolve:

  • Astrologers you consult see your name, the birth details you provide, and your consultation messages and calls.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf:
    • Google Firebase — phone login, push notifications, and analytics
    • Razorpay — payments and refunds
    • Stream — chat messaging
    • Agora — in-app voice calls and call recording
    • Exotel — connecting consultations over phone calls
    • Prokerala — astrology calculations for horoscopes and kundli charts
    • Amazon Web Services — hosting and file storage
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.
  • Emergency services or authorities, to prevent serious harm, if a consultation indicates that someone may be at risk of harming themselves or others. AstroSolve is not a mental-health or crisis service. If you are in distress, contact emergency services (112) or a mental-health helpline.
  • A successor business, if AstroSolve is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside India. We use them only for the purposes above and require them to protect your data.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Call recordings: 90 days after the consultation.
  • Chat messages: 1 year after the consultation.
  • Payment, wallet, and billing records: 8 years, as required for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Security and device logs: only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

We may keep specific data longer if it is needed for an ongoing dispute, investigation, or legal requirement.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app, or by request. See Delete your account for the steps. When you delete your account we erase your name, phone number, email, profile photo, birth details, kundli charts, and saved preferences, and we sign you out on all devices. Payment and billing records are kept for the period above without your name, phone number, or email. Reviews you posted remain without your name. If wallet balance remains, you may request a refund of unused paid balance through customer support before deletion, or confirm in the app that you are giving up the displayed total balance. Deletion is temporarily unavailable while a payment or refund is processing. Bonus credit is not refundable. See our Refund Policy for details.

Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Correct or update inaccurate data. Most details can be edited in the app's profile section.
  • Ask us to erase your data, or delete your account yourself.
  • Withdraw consent. This may mean we can no longer provide the service to you.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email us from your registered details using the contact below.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls limited to authorised staff, and secure storage with our service providers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

This website

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts, which may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type. If you submit the astrologer waitlist form, your name, email, mobile number, and expertise are stored in Google Forms and used only to contact you about onboarding.

Severability

If any part of this policy is found invalid or unenforceable, the rest of it continues to apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and notify you in the app for significant changes.
