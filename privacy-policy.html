Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

AstroSolve ("we", "us", "our") is currently in a pre-launch phase. This policy explains what little information we collect today, ahead of our full product launch, and how we handle it.

What we collect

The only data we currently collect is what you voluntarily submit through the astrologer waitlist form on this site: your name, email address, mobile number, and area of astrological expertise. This form is hosted by Google Forms, and submissions are stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to the AstroSolve team.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts) may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and page requests. We do not run any analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

Waitlist information is used solely to contact prospective astrologers about onboarding onto the AstroSolve platform when we open access. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your waitlist submission at any time by emailing us using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

We will publish a complete privacy policy covering account data, payments, and consultations when the AstroSolve app launches. This page will be updated at that time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to founder@astrosolve.co.
